# Sevan 70.3 · Race Day Nutrition Plan

**Race day: Sunday September 6, 2026 · Lake Sevan, Armenia**

## Target: 60 g carbs per hour of racing

~350 g total across the ~6-hour race, plus pre-race breakfast.

| When | What |

| Time in | Action |

| 0:20 | Gel 1 + sip water |

**Bike setup**

- Bottle 1: sports drink (60 g carbs, 500–700 mg sodium)
- Bottle 2: water
- 4–5 gels taped to top tube, caffeinated one at position 3

## Run Leg — Target ~2 h · ~100 g carbs

Small, frequent, mostly liquid. GI stress is higher on the run.

| Distance | Action |
|---|---|
| Km 3 (~15 min) | Gel 1 + water at aid station |
| Km 7 (~35 min) | Water only, sponge/ice on neck |
| Km 10 (~55 min) | **Gel 2 (caffeinated)** + water |
| Km 13 (~75 min) | Cola at aid station (usually flat, easier on stomach) |

## Hydration and Electrolytes

- **Water:** 400–600 ml/hour, more if hot (Sevan can hit 25–28°C by late morning in September)
- **Sodium:** 400–600 mg/hour through sports drink and electrolyte tabs — dilute one tab per water bottle refill
- **Under-fueling signs:** dry mouth, elevated HR at same pace, mental fog
- **Over-fueling signs:** bloating, sloshing stomach, nausea. Adjust down.

---

## Caffeine Rules

- **Exactly 2 caffeinated gels total.** Spaced 90+ minutes apart.
- Timing: one late bike (1:30 in), one early run (55 min in)
- **Do NOT skip the mid-bike caffeine** — it's timed so you start the run with caffeine already active in the bloodstream, not chasing it

---

## Gel and Brand Rules

- **Never race-day anything untested.** Whatever you use on Saturday's brick is what you use on Sep 6.
- Recommended: Maurten (isotonic, easy on stomach). SIS is also good.
- **Backup:** carry 1 extra gel in the run belt beyond what the plan calls for. Better to have and not need.

## Race-Week Non-Training Checklist

- **Thu Sep 3 evening:** begin structured carb-loading — aim 7–10 g/kg body weight of carbs per day through Saturday. Reduce fat and fibre.
- **Fri Sep 4:** pack race bag with a written checklist. Fresh chamois cream, gels labelled with times, spare tube, CO2, tire lever.
- **Fri night:** normal bedtime. Do not try to sleep early — you'll just lie awake.
- **Sat Sep 5:** travel to Sevan. Ride 10 min on the bike to confirm nothing has shifted. Rack bike in T1. Walk swim exit and transition zones. Practice water bottle grab.
- **Sat night:** lights out by 22:00. Two alarms.
- **Race morning:** eat 2.5–3 hours before start (200 g carbs, low fibre — porridge with honey and banana is the safe choice). Coffee. Warm up with 10 min easy jog + strides.
