## The finding that matters: your run collapses off the bike

Your standalone half marathon best is **1:49:22, which is 5:11/km.** In these four races you ran 7:13, 6:52, 6:41 and 6:48 per kilometre — between **90 and 120 seconds per kilometre slower** than your open half pace. A normal off-the-bike penalty is 5–10%. Yours is 30–40%. That is not a pacing quirk, it is the defining feature of your racing, and it has cost you the same 15–20 minutes four years running.

## Why it is not a fitness problem

| Race | Bike avg HR | Run avg HR | Run pace | Reading |

| 2022 | 155 | 158 | 7:13/km | Fastest bike of the four, slowest run |
| 2023 | 153 | 156 | 6:52/km | Hilliest bike at 815 m of climb |
| 2024 | 155 | 157 | 6:41/km | Best run, and the overall PB |
| 2025 | 150 | 160 | 6:48/km | Lowest bike HR, highest run HR |

Your lactate threshold heart rate is 171, and in your standalone half marathon you averaged 171 for the full distance. In these races your run heart rate never exceeded 160. You are finishing these half marathons **eleven or more beats below threshold** — you are not running out of cardiovascular capacity, you are arriving at T2 with legs that will not answer.

## The prescription this points to

Bike heart rate of 150–155 is roughly 88–91% of your run threshold, held for three hours. That is too high for a race with a half marathon attached, and the run splits are the receipt. **Target 140–145 on the bike this year.** You will lose perhaps three or four minutes on a bike leg that is already your strongest discipline, and stand to gain 10–15 on a run leg that has underperformed every single year. This is exactly why the marathon-first restructure helps here: more run volume and actual brick sessions attack the one weakness these four races all agree on.

## What I got wrong two days ago

- I estimated your run at 2:02 by applying a textbook 8% off-the-bike penalty to your standalone half PR.
- Your actual penalty across four races is 30–40%. The real number is 2:10–2:20.
- The bike estimate held up well — I predicted 30–33 km/h from power, and you have ridden 30–32 in every race.
- Net effect: total moves from 5:55 to roughly 6:00–6:10.

## Where the minutes actually are

- **Run, 10–15 min.** Ride easier, train bricks, raise run volume. By far the largest lever.
- **Swim, 5 min.** Your 2022 and 2024 swims were six minutes faster than 2023 and 2025 — consistency alone is worth time.
- **Transitions, 1–2 min.** Cheapest minutes in the sport and you have never rehearsed them.
- **Bike, roughly zero.** It is already good. Do not chase time here.
